03 — DOCTORAL RESEARCH

EHR adoption & behavioural intentions

01

Saveetha University · 2021–2026

Behavioural, organizational and psychological determinants of EHR adoption

  • Quantitative study involving 420+ healthcare professionals across multi-specialty hospitals in Chennai.
  • Nine-construct SEM model using AMOS, integrating social influence, self-efficacy, performance expectancy, perceived usefulness, perceived risk and behavioural intention.
  • Translated findings into recommendations to reduce digital adoption barriers and strengthen staff readiness for EHR implementation.
  • Contributed to research ethics submissions and proposal development and mentored junior researchers in quantitative methods, AMOS-SEM and academic writing.
