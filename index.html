Doctoral Research — EHR Adoption & Behavioural Intentions

Saveetha University, India · 2021–2026

  • Quantitative study involving 420+ healthcare professionals across multi-specialty hospitals in Chennai.
  • Nine-construct SEM model using AMOS integrating social influence, self-efficacy, performance expectancy, perceived usefulness, perceived risk and behavioural intention.
  • Model fit reported as CFI > 0.95 and RMSEA < 0.08.
  • Findings translated into evidence-based recommendations to reduce digital adoption barriers and strengthen staff readiness for EHR implementation.
